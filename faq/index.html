Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about TV Boost Pro.

TV Boost Pro is an optimization and analysis tool designed for Android TV devices. It scans your TV to provide insights on storage usage, identifies unused or resource-heavy apps, lets you adjust system animation speeds for a snappier feel, and generates an overall health score for your device. Think of it as a dashboard that helps you understand and maintain your TV's performance.
Yes, TV Boost Pro is completely safe. All analysis and processing happens locally on your device — no data is sent to external servers. The app does not contain any active analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not access your personal files, photos, browsing history, or any sensitive information. The app only reads system-level metrics (storage stats, app list, usage data) needed for its optimization features.
TV Boost Pro does not collect or transmit any data. The app accesses storage metrics, your installed app list, app usage statistics (with your permission), and animation settings — but all of this information stays on your device. There is no cloud backend, no user accounts, and no data sharing with third parties. Please see our Privacy Policy for full details.
The health score is a composite rating calculated from several factors including available storage space, the number of unused apps, cache buildup, app usage patterns, and animation settings. A higher score indicates that your TV is well-maintained with adequate free storage and minimal unnecessary apps. The score is recalculated each time you run a scan, and weekly health reports track how it changes over time.
The Pro subscription unlocks advanced features including detailed storage breakdown, full app usage analytics, the animation optimizer tool, weekly automated health reports, advanced mode settings, and an ad-free experience. The free version provides basic scanning and a limited health score overview.
TV Boost Pro offers three plans: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription (with a significant discount compared to monthly), and a one-time lifetime purchase. Exact pricing is displayed in the app and on Google Play, as it may vary by region. Visit our Subscriptions page for more details on each plan.
To cancel your subscription, open the Google Play Store on any device signed into the same Google account, go to Menu > Subscriptions, find TV Boost Pro, and select "Cancel subscription." You will retain access to Pro features until the end of your current billing period. Uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel your subscription — you must cancel through Google Play.
TV Boost Pro supports most Android TV and Google TV devices running Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher. This includes devices from Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi, NVIDIA Shield, and Chromecast with Google TV. Some features may be limited on certain devices due to manufacturer-specific restrictions. The app is optimized for TV remote (D-pad) navigation.
TV Boost Pro is designed and optimized specifically for Android TV devices. While the app may install on some Android phones or tablets, the user interface and features are tailored for the TV experience and large-screen remote-controlled navigation. For the best experience, we recommend using it on an Android TV device.
The storage analysis uses Android's built-in system APIs to read storage metrics, so it reflects the same information your device reports. However, some storage may be reserved by the system or manufacturer and is not visible to third-party apps. The "Other" or "System" category may include files that cannot be individually listed due to Android's security restrictions. Overall, the analysis provides an accurate and useful overview of how your storage is being used.
The Animation Optimizer adjusts three system animation scales: window animation, transition animation, and animator duration. Reducing these values makes your TV's interface respond faster by shortening or eliminating the visual transitions between screens and actions. This does not change your TV's actual processing power but creates a noticeably snappier feel. You can always restore the default settings through the app.
The weekly health check is a Pro feature that automatically evaluates your TV's status once per week. It recalculates your health score and provides a summary of changes since the last check, such as storage usage trends, new app installations, or cache growth. This helps you stay on top of your TV's condition without having to manually scan each time.
TV Boost Pro supports multiple languages including English, Turkish, German, French, and Spanish. The app automatically uses your device's language setting. If your device language is not supported, the app defaults to English. We plan to add more languages in future updates based on user demand.
Advanced Mode is a Pro feature that provides deeper system-level insights and more granular control over optimization settings. It includes detailed per-app storage breakdowns, precise animation scale adjustments, and additional diagnostic information. Advanced Mode is designed for users who want more control over their TV's configuration. It is entirely optional and the standard mode covers most users' needs.
