Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Key point: TV Boost Pro processes all data locally on your device. No personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TV Boost Pro ("the App"), developed by DEVELOPER_LEGAL_NAME_REQUIRED ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use our Android TV optimization application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Data We Access

To provide its optimization features, TV Boost Pro accesses the following types of information on your device:

  • Storage Information: The App reads general storage metrics (total space, used space, available space) and app-level storage consumption to provide storage analysis and recommendations.
  • Installed App List: The App reads the list of installed applications on your device to display app management features and identify unused applications.
  • App Usage Statistics: With your explicit permission (Usage Access permission), the App reads app usage data to determine which apps you use frequently and which remain idle. This permission is optional and the App functions without it.
  • Animation Settings: The App reads current system animation scale values (window animation, transition animation, animator duration) to provide the animation optimization feature.

3. On-Device Processing

All data accessed by TV Boost Pro is processed entirely on your device. Specifically:

  • Storage analysis is computed locally using Android system APIs.
  • Health scores are calculated on-device using locally available metrics.
  • App usage statistics are read and displayed locally; they are never transmitted.
  • Weekly health reports are generated and stored on your device only.
  • No data is uploaded to any cloud server, remote database, or external service.

No Analytics SDK: The current version of TV Boost Pro does not include any active analytics or tracking SDK. While the app contains an analytics interface for potential future use, it is implemented as a no-operation (NoOp) — meaning no analytics data is collected or sent anywhere.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

TV Boost Pro does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Browsing history
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Any form of user-generated content

5. No Cloud Backend

TV Boost Pro operates without a cloud backend. There are no user accounts, no remote servers storing your data, and no cloud synchronization. The App is designed as a fully offline tool that works entirely within your device's local environment.

6. Billing and Purchases

If you choose to subscribe to TV Boost Pro's premium features, all billing is managed entirely by Google Play. We do not process, store, or have access to your payment information, credit card details, or billing address. For billing-related privacy information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Since no data leaves your device, there is nothing to share. We do not engage in:

  • Selling data to advertisers or data brokers
  • Sharing data with analytics providers
  • Transmitting data to any affiliated or unaffiliated third parties

8. Children's Privacy

TV Boost Pro is a general utility application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since the App does not collect any personal information from any user, no special provisions for children's data are necessary.

9. International Users

TV Boost Pro is available globally through Google Play. Because all data processing occurs locally on your device, no data crosses international borders through our App. Your data remains on your device regardless of your geographic location.

10. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device and is never transmitted externally, the security of your information is governed by your device's own security measures. We recommend keeping your Android TV's software up to date and using a secure home network to maintain device-level security.

11. Data Retention

TV Boost Pro stores application preferences and cached analysis results locally on your device. This data persists until you clear the app's data, uninstall the application, or reset your device. We do not retain any data on external servers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
