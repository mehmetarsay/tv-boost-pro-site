Auto-Renewal Terms

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are auto-renewing. Your Google Play account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

After purchasing a subscription, you can manage it and turn off auto-renewal in your Google Play Store account settings. The subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period after cancellation.

Cancellation Process

You can cancel your subscription at any time by following these steps:

  1. Open the Google Play Store on any device signed into the same Google account.
  2. Tap your profile icon and go to Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  3. Find TV Boost Pro and tap it.
  4. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the prompts.

After cancellation, you will continue to have access to Pro features until the end of your current paid period. Once the period ends, your account will revert to the free version.

Important: Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. You must cancel through Google Play to stop future charges.

Refund Policy

Refund requests are handled by Google Play in accordance with their refund policies. To request a refund:

  • Visit Google Play's refund page.
  • Google Play typically allows refunds within 48 hours of purchase for subscriptions.
  • For the lifetime purchase, Google Play's standard digital content refund policy applies.
  • If you have billing concerns, you can also contact us at SUPPORT_EMAIL_REQUIRED and we will assist where possible.

Free Trial

If a free trial is offered, it will be clearly indicated on the subscription screen in the app. The trial provides full Pro access for the trial duration. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will automatically convert to a paid subscription at the displayed rate. Free trial availability and duration may vary.
